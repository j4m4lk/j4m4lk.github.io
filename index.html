About me

Senior Game Developer with 7+ years of experience building and shipping games in Unity and Unreal Engine 5.

Strong in C# and C++, specialising in gameplay programming, scalable architecture, multiplayer replication, and data-driven world building, including real-world city generation from CSV data. Skilled in performance optimisation across CPU, memory, GC, and draw calls, and in building custom editor and pipeline tools. Experienced in taking games from prototype to launch. Uses AI-assisted workflows for faster coding, testing, and profiling, cutting development and debugging time by half.

I also build my own AI-assisted tools, such as a Unity level design tool that reuses texture atlases, shows draw calls as you build, and snaps and duplicates objects to keep draw calls down. These days I'm modelling assets in Blender for my own game and bringing them into Unity through a Blender-to-Unity pipeline.

Kreed Digital Studio

An independent two-person game studio.

Kreed began in Islamabad, where Burhan Ahmad and I met, and now works between the UK and Dubai. I'm the game developer and programmer; Burhan is a game and XR developer.

Our first game is Rook's Quest, a blitz chess roguelite set in Karachi, made in Unity and coming to Steam. I model its assets in Blender and use AI-assisted tools to speed up coding, testing and profiling.
